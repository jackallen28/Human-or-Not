Welcome

Students chat with either a classmate or an AI pretending to be one, then guess which. Round by round the AI studies your class and gets harder to catch — or you play on and swap the models yourself. Here is the whole set-up:

Three ways to play

Three rounds, learning. The latest Claude Haiku, Sonnet and Opus every round, shared evenly among the students paired with an AI. After each round, Opus reads every conversation and teaches the AI what gave it away.

Four rounds, learning, then a switch. The same three learning rounds, then a fourth with the models you choose — by default Opus quietly switched off. Students are not told.

Open play. A few rounds at a time, as many as you like. No learning between rounds — instead you choose which models play each round.
